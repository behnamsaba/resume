Time Zoner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers the Time Zoner mobile app, developed by Ben Saba. The app has no account system, advertising, or analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not automatically send your favorites, reminders, settings, or calendar contents to us.

Information on your device

Time Zoner stores your favorite time zones, reminders, calendar and holiday choices, and other preferences on your device so they are available when you reopen the app. It uses your device's time zone setting to display local times; it does not request GPS or precise location access.

The optional home-screen widget receives a local cache of dates and holidays based on your calendar and place selections. This information is shared with the widget on your device, not sent to us.

Calendar access and notifications

With your permission, Time Zoner reads events from your device's calendars to show upcoming events in your time zones. When you choose to add an event, it opens the system calendar editor for you to review and save it. Events saved to a calendar may sync with that calendar's provider, such as iCloud or Google, under your account settings and that provider's privacy policy.

Reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. Notification content, including reminder titles, may appear on your lock screen according to your device settings. You can change notification previews and revoke calendar or notification permissions in your device's Settings at any time. The features requiring that permission will then be limited.

Sharing, links, and contacting us

If you use Share, the information you select is passed to the app or recipient you choose. Opening an external link or contacting us by email uses your browser or email service. Those services handle information according to their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use this correspondence to respond to your request and provide support. Please include only information needed to explain your question or problem.

Keeping and deleting information

Local preferences and reminders remain on your device until you change or remove them, or delete the app and its data. Offloading the app may preserve its data. Device backups may also retain a copy; backup, restoration, and deletion are controlled by your device and backup provider's settings.

Deleting Time Zoner does not delete events you saved to a calendar or copies you shared with other apps or people. Manage those copies in the relevant calendar or service. We cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on your device.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to address your request and meet applicable obligations. To ask us to delete correspondence or other personal information you have sent us, email ben@bensaba.dev. We will respond to your request and explain any information we must retain.

Visiting this policy page

Loading this page sends standard web request information, such as your IP address, browser information, and the requested address, to the website's hosting infrastructure. Hosting providers may process that information to deliver and protect the page. This page itself does not set cookies or load analytics scripts, external fonts, or other external assets.

Changes and questions

If the app's practices change, we will update this policy and its date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Ben Saba at ben@bensaba.dev.
